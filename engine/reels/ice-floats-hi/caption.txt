# Why ice floats, in Hinglish: the scenes of ice-floats, cued through reel.json "cues".
== hook | ground: dark
Thanda paani neeche / *doob* jaata hai.
Toh phir *frozen* paani
float karke upar kyun rehta hai?
== lake | ground: dark
Dekho ek *lake* ko, autumn mein.
Upar ka surface water / thanda hota hai,
*bhaari* hota hai, / aur doob jaata hai.
Ye tab tak chalta hai,
jab tak poori lake lagbhag
*chaar* degree pe na aa jaaye.
Phir rule *palat* jaata hai.
Chaar degree se neeche,
paani thanda hone pe / *halka* hota jaata hai.
Sabse thanda paani / upar rehta hai,
aur wahin *ice* banti hai.
== liquid | ground: dark
Kyun? Ye dekhne ke liye / *zoom* in karo.
Liquid paani mein molecules
ek doosre ke paas se
*ludhakte* hue nikalte hain.
Unke beech ke weak bonds
toot jaate hain
aur phir se ban jaate hain,
ek second ke *trillionth*
hisse se bhi kam mein.
Isliye kuch bhi / ek jagah *ruka* nahi rehta.
== cage | ground: dark
Inhe freeze karo, aur har / *oxygen* apne chaar
padosiyon se jud jaata hai.
Ye bonds milkar / *rings* banate hain,
har ring mein chheh,
aur ye layer pe layer / jam jaate hain.
== roomy | ground: dark
Ye *cage* khula hua hai.
Molecules ab zyada *door* baithte hain,
liquid se bhi zyada door.
Toh wahi paani ab leta hai,
lagbhag nau *percent* / zyada jagah.
== payoff | ground: dark
Wahi paani, zyada *jagah.*
Isliye ice apne aas paas ke
paani se *halki* ho jaati hai,
aur upar aa jaati hai.
Ye tairta hua *dhakkan*
neeche ke paani ko insulate karta hai,
isliye talaab aksar
poore nahi *jamte.*
Ice isliye tairti hai,
kyunki jamte waqt paani
khulkar ek *cage* ban jaata hai,
jo usi paani se halka hai
jisse wo bana.
