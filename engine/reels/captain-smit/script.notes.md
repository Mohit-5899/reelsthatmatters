# Script notes: captain-smit

Hook: hooks.md #1 (the myth broken), used word for word. 188 words (wc -w; hyphenated words count as one), about 59 s at 3.2 words a second.
Every sentence rests on a High fact in facts.md. No Medium fact is used. The co-pilot is not named; no motive, weapon or injury detail.

## Paragraph 1: the myth
- "A locked cockpit door keeps a plane safe." The myth, as written in facts.md "The myth" [1][14].
- "Unless the danger is inside." The myth section; facts 9, 25.

## Paragraph 2: why doors are built that way
- "Cockpit doors are built to keep attackers out." The myth section ("Reinforced doors are built to keep attackers out") [1][14].
- "Under American rules, a door must hold against being forced, and stop handgun bullets." Fact 25 (High: 14 CFR 25.795). Worded as "American rules" because the source is a US regulation; the script does not claim this flydubai door was certified to it. Visual should show a generic door, not "FZ1073's door".
- "Its lock opens only from inside." Fact 25 (High). Fact 26 (keypad, either seat) is Medium and is left out.

## Paragraph 3: what happened
- "On the thirtieth of September, flydubai flight ten seventy-three left Dubai for Tel Aviv." Fact 1.
- "More than two hours in, over Saudi Arabia near Jordan, the co-pilot attacked the captain inside the locked cockpit." Facts 6, 9, 1 (flight deck). **Disputed timing:** about 2 h 15 min (ToI) vs nearly 2.5 h (Al Jazeera); "more than two hours" is true under both.
- "The jet dived, falling more than fifteen thousand feet in under two minutes." Fact 7. **Disputed dive:** 34,000 to about 17,000 ft in about a minute (17,000 ft) vs to about 19,000 ft in about 90 s (15,000 ft); "more than fifteen thousand feet in under two minutes" is true under both. The script does not say who or what caused the dive.

## Paragraph 4: the captain and the door
- "The captain was Smit Machchhar, from Mumbai." Fact 3.
- "He was seriously injured." Fact 16 ("seriously injured"); fact 10 ("badly wounded"). No detail of the injuries (fact 9 left out on purpose).
- "But the cockpit door came open, and help got in." Facts 10, 11. **Disputed door:** Israeli officials say the captain released it from inside (fact 10); the UAE AG says a return-flight pilot saw it "opening and closing repeatedly" and entered with the access code (fact 11). "The door came open, and help got in" is true under both, and does not say the captain personally unlocked it.
- "Crew and passengers held the attacker down." Facts 11 (a flydubai pilot, one cabin crew member, several passengers restrained him), 12 (Israeli passengers helped overpower him). "Crew and passengers" covers both accounts.

## Paragraph 5: the landing
- "Two flydubai pilots on board for the return flight took the controls." Fact 13. **Disputed wording:** "off-duty pilots" (Israel), "on-duty flydubai crew travelling on the flight" (flydubai), "return-flight captain and his co-pilot" (UAE AG). "On board for the return flight" avoids saying on-duty or off-duty.
- "About an hour later, they landed safely at Tabuk, in Saudi Arabia." Fact 14 (about an hour later, about 85 min from cruise); "safely" from the one-line answer and fact 15.
- "flydubai said every passenger was safe." Fact 15, attributed to flydubai. **Not claimed:** "everyone on board survived". facts.md says all passengers safe (15), the captain recovering (16) and the co-pilot held (17), but has no explicit statement covering every crew member, so the script limits itself to flydubai's wording. The head count is also left out (172 to 180 across sources).

## Paragraph 6: after
- "The captain was treated in Tabuk, and the Indian Embassy said he was recovering well." Fact 16 (statement around 2 to 3 Oct; facts.md found no later update, so the script keeps it in the past tense and attributed).
- "India's Prime Minister called him a hero." Fact 22 (High). The Order of Zayed (fact 24) is Medium and is left out. Netanyahu's "174 people" is left out because the head count is disputed.

## Paragraph 7: the close
- "A locked door is meant to keep danger out." The myth section; fact 25.
- "On this flight, the door that opened is what saved the plane." The one-line answer and the myth section ("In this case, opening the door saved the plane"). It credits the opening of the door, not one person's action, so it holds under both door accounts.
