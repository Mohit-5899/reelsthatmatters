# Blinkit: a 30 s Hinglish spec ad (unofficial concept, not commissioned by Blinkit)

## The one-line answer
Blinkit is the app for last-minute needs: 30,000+ products, from milk to printouts to pet food, delivered to your doorstep.

## Claims we must NOT make
- No delivery-time promise ("10 minutes", "12 minutes"). Blinkit dropped its 10-minute claim on 13 Jan 2026 after the Labour Ministry raised gig-worker safety concerns; the tagline became "30,000+ products delivered at your doorstep". [3][4][5] (High: several outlets agree)
- No rider racing, speeding or a countdown clock on a delivery. Fast *editing* is fine; fast *riding* is not.
- No Blinkit logo file, no copied ad artwork. Name in plain type only.

## Facts
1. Blinkit's homepage title reads "30,000+ products delivered to your doorstep | Blinkit". [1] (High: read live in Chrome, 1 Nov 2026)
2. Categories on the site include Vegetables & Fruits; Dairy, Bread & Eggs; Chips & Namkeen; Baby Care; Health & Pharma; Pet care; Print Store (printouts); Electronics; E-Gift Cards. Banners: "Get printouts delivered", "Pharmacy at your doorstep", "Pet care supplies at your door", "No time for a diaper run?". [1] (High)
3. Blinkit is owned by Blink Commerce Private Limited, part of Eternal (formerly Zomato). [1][2] (High)
4. Blinkit has used the line "India's Last Minute App" on a yellow billboard (Maybelline tie-up). [6] (Medium: one source, undated)
5. Blinkit had 2,243 dark stores on 31 Mar 2026. [2] (Medium: brokerage note on Eternal's Q4 FY26) — not used in the ad.

## Look
Site palette: yellow wordmark, green buttons and banners, white product cards. [1] (observed)
